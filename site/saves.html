How it handles your saves.

Your saves stay on your PC, and nothing is replaced until a copy of it has been read back.

Where it all is.

Each slot's files
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lootnest\profiles
Copies before swaps
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lootnest\backups
Copies of every save
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lootnest\safety-net
Save points
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lootnest\savepoints

Stays shared.

  • Steam achievements and playtime
  • Friends, inventory, online ranks
  • Settings kept in the registry or online

If something goes wrong.

  • The PC turns off mid-swap. It's finished the next time Lootnest starts.
  • Steam Cloud replaces a slot's files. The game's page says so; the slot is kept as it was.
  • You want an old copy back. The game's page, Copies before swaps, Put back.
