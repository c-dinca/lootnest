# Privacy

No account and no telemetry; Lootnest works offline. It only goes online when you ask it to:

| What | When | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Check for updates** | When you press it, or every 6 hours if **Check for updates automatically** is on (off until you answer "Keep Lootnest up to date?") | A request for the latest Lootnest version, to `api.github.com` |
| **Update now** | When you press it | Downloads the installer from the GitHub release, and checks its SHA-256 before running it |
| **Download covers and artwork** | Only if you turn it on (off by default) | The game's name, to `store.steampowered.com`; covers, wide art and logos come from Steam's image server and are saved on your PC |
| **SteamGridDB API key** (optional, in Settings) | With covers on, for games the Steam store doesn't know | The game's name or Steam id and your key, to `www.steamgriddb.com`; images come from its CDN and are saved on your PC |
| **Steam Web API key** (optional, in Settings) | When you open a game's page and its achievements on this PC have no names (a game in a folder, or Steam's partial list); each game's list is kept for a week | The game's Steam app id and your key, to `api.steampowered.com`; the names, descriptions and picture links are saved on your PC |
| **How rare achievements are** (Settings, Online) | Only if you turn it on (off by default), when a game's achievements are shown or one unlocks; each game's numbers are kept for three days | The game's Steam app id, to `api.steampowered.com` (Steam's public global percentages; no key) |
| **Show game updates and news** | Only if you turn it on (off by default), when you open a game's page; each feed is read at most every 6 hours | The game's Steam app id to `api.steampowered.com` (for a game outside Steam, first its name to `store.steampowered.com`); an update's pictures load from Steam's image server when you open it |
| **Game details** (Settings, Online) | Only if you turn it on (off by default), when you open a game's page and the copy on your PC is missing or over 30 days old | The game's Steam app id, to `store.steampowered.com` (its summary, genres, developer, publisher, release date, Metacritic score and Steam review score); screenshots load from Steam's image server. When you scroll down to Community, the same app id asks for the most helpful reviews of the past year (kept in memory for six hours, never on your PC); nothing is posted. Your own edits stay on your PC |
| **Store** (Settings, Online) | Only if you turn it on (off by default), when you open the Store page, search in it or in the top bar, or open a game there | What you type, to `store.steampowered.com` (with the country Windows is set to, for prices in your currency); a game's Steam id, to `store.steampowered.com` and `www.cheapshark.com`. Your PC's processor, graphics card and memory are read on your PC and never sent |
| **Find save locations online** (Settings, Online, or Cloud saves) | Only if you turn it on (off by default), then about once a week | Nothing about you or your games: Lootnest downloads the Ludusavi project's list of where games keep their saves (from PCGamingWiki) from `raw.githubusercontent.com` and keeps it on your PC |
| **itch.io** (Settings, Online) | Only when you connect it with your own API key: the library once per start and when you refresh it, a game's downloads when you install it, and a check for an update when you open the game's page or Updates (at most every 6 hours) or press Check | Your key, to `api.itch.io`, to read your library and your games' downloads; a game you install or update downloads from itch.io's download servers. Your API key is kept encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) |
| **GOG** (Settings, Online) | Only when you sign in, in your browser on GOG's own page: the library once per start and when you refresh it, a game's installers when you install it, and a check for an update when you open the game's page or Updates (at most every 6 hours) or press Check | The sign-in, to `auth.gog.com`, `embed.gog.com` and `api.gog.com`, to read your user name, your library and your games' installers; a game you install or update downloads from GOG's download servers. The sign-in's refresh token is kept encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) and never shown to the page |
| **Import from Playnite** (Settings, Data and activity) | Only when you press it | Nothing goes online: Lootnest reads Playnite's library files on your PC and never changes them |
| **Captures from other apps** (Settings, Data and activity) | On by default, when the Library is scanned and when a game closes, at most every 10 minutes | Nothing goes online: Lootnest reads the screenshot and clip folders of Game Bar, Steam, NVIDIA, AMD and OBS on your PC to show them on each game's Timeline, and never moves, renames or deletes a capture |

**Cloud saves and backup** never go online themselves: Lootnest writes the backup file to the folder you choose. If that folder is one OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox syncs, that app uploads it, under that app's own terms. With a password, the file is encrypted on your PC first (AES-256-GCM), so the cloud only ever has the encrypted file.
